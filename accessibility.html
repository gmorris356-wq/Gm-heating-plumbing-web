Accessibility Statement

1. Our aim

GM Heating & Plumbing aims to make its website usable by as many people as reasonably practicable. The site uses semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, a skip link, responsive layouts and readable contrast.

2. Known limitations

This is a small business website rather than a public-sector service, and some content or third-party material may not meet every accessibility requirement. If you encounter a barrier, please tell us what you were trying to do and what went wrong.

3. Contact

Email gmheatingplumbing@gmail.com or call 07988 150927. We will consider reasonable ways to provide information or communicate with you in an accessible format.
