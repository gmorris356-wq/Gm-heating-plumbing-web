COOKIES & ONLINE STORAGE

Cookie & Storage Notice

1. What this website uses

The public website is designed to operate without advertising cookies, analytics cookies or tracking pixels. No non-essential cookie is intentionally set by the public pages in this package.

2. Essential storage

The separate employee portal previously used browser storage for a demo login and demo records. That portal has been locked in this publication version. No live customer information should be stored in browser localStorage/sessionStorage.

3. Third-party services

The publication version removes the Google Fonts dependency from the public website to reduce unnecessary third-party requests. If analytics, maps, chat, advertising, embedded media or other third-party technologies are added later, their storage/access behaviour must be reviewed and an appropriate consent mechanism must be implemented before non-essential technologies are activated.

4. Your choices

You can control cookies and similar technologies through your browser. If the site later introduces non-essential technologies, this notice and the consent controls will be updated.
