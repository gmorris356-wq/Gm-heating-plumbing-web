CUSTOMER SERVICE TERMS

Customer Service Terms

Important: These are baseline consumer-service terms for review and tailoring. The final quotation/order should take priority for job-specific scope, price, timing and exclusions.

1. Scope and acceptance

Work will be carried out according to the agreed quotation, job description and any written variations. Additional work should be agreed before it is undertaken where reasonably practicable.

2. Pricing

Prices should clearly identify whether VAT is included, where applicable, and should distinguish labour, materials, call-out charges, specialist subcontractors and other agreed costs. Unforeseen work may require a revised quotation.

3. Access and customer responsibilities

The customer must provide reasonable access, relevant information about the property and any known hazards or defects. We may pause work where conditions are unsafe or where required information, permissions or access are unavailable.

4. Materials and workmanship

Materials will be suitable for the agreed work subject to availability and manufacturer requirements. Work will be carried out with reasonable care and skill and in accordance with applicable legal and technical requirements.

5. Urgent repairs and cancellation

Consumer cancellation rights can apply to contracts made at a distance or off-premises. Where the law gives a cancellation period, customers will be informed of the applicable rights and any lawful exceptions. If a customer expressly asks for work to begin during a cancellation period, the required information and consent should be obtained. Emergency situations and work already fully performed may be subject to specific statutory rules and exceptions.

6. Payment

Payment terms, due dates and accepted methods will be stated in the quotation or invoice. Customers should receive appropriate invoices and records. Any late-payment rights will be exercised only where lawful and appropriate.

7. Defects, guarantees and manufacturer warranties

Any workmanship guarantee offered by GT will be stated in writing. Manufacturer warranties remain subject to the manufacturer's terms and registration requirements. Nothing in these terms removes statutory consumer rights.

8. Complaints

Please contact us promptly with details of the concern so we can investigate and, where appropriate, put matters right. Keep quotations, invoices, photographs and relevant correspondence as useful evidence.

9. Safety, qualifications and regulated work

GM Heating & Plumbing only accepts work that falls within the current competence, training, insurance and legal permissions of the person carrying it out. Regulated gas work and specialist installations outside this scope are not currently offered. Where specialist work is required, the customer will be advised to use an appropriately qualified and registered professional.

10. Consumer rights

These terms are intended to operate alongside the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and other applicable UK consumer legislation. If any term conflicts with a mandatory consumer right, the mandatory right prevails.

Last reviewed: 12 August 2026. Obtain legal review before relying on these terms as a final consumer contract.
